# Preservation records, release 2.2

The collections chapter adds three dated treatment examples and two studies of how manuscript objects acquired their present form. The six references are kept in `dist/preservation-data.js`, separately from the map and Gephi snapshots. The in-page download exports this same bilingual evidence dataset.

## Verification and access

Source screening used the existing local Chinese reading PDFs and the NUS-downloaded Dotson/Doney chapter, supplemented by institutional reports. NLC’s 2023 PDF was read and its illustrated treatment page inspected. The local Xu and Dotson/Doney pages were inspected visually. The IDP News33 PDF full text was available through the web reader; direct download returned 403 and its image rendering was unavailable. The public page reproduces no report photographs or licensed full texts. Raw searches, extraction files, local PDFs and access limitations remain in the private project research directory.

The 2025 NLC publication report is used for its dated institutional account, not its website copyright year. Older conservation practices are not described as universal current practice. A 1998 BnF report partly concerning Kucha material was screened out of the Dunhuang comparison. Doumy’s 2025 discussion of substantial scrolls retained by Wang Yuanlu also prevents a blanket claim that all complete manuscripts were taken abroad.

All six references include author, year, title, URL and locators in the downloadable evidence. No additional NUS authentication was needed for the already-downloaded chapter and Chinese PDFs. There is no change to the historical 1900–1910 scope, graph topology, directions, Gephi layout or GEXF.
